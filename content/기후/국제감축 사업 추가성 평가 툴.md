## CDM
- **[Tool for the demonstration and assessment of additionality" (AM-TOOL-01)** ](https://cdm.unfccc.int/methodologies/PAmethodologies/tools/am-tool-01-v7.0.0.pdf)— is **Version 7.0.0** / ==일반, 대규모 프로젝트용==
- A few things worth knowing alongside that:
	- There's a related, frequently-used companion tool, **TOOL02 – "[Combined tool to identify the baseline scenario and demonstrate additionality](https://cdm.unfccc.int/methodologies/PAmethodologies/tools/am-tool-02-v7.0.pdf),"** which many methodologies reference together with TOOL01. 
	- For small-scale and micro-scale projects, the CDM has separate tools (**[TOOL19](https://cdm.unfccc.int/Reference/catalogue/document?doc_id=000004349)** for microscale, **[TOOL21]([cat quartz.config.yaml | grep -A10 "content-meta"](https://cdm.unfccc.int/methodologies/PAmethodologies/tools/am-tool-21-v13.1.pdf))** for small-scale additionality), and for afforestation/reforestation there's a distinct **AR-AM-TOOL-01**. 
		- TOOL21 : 소규모 
		- TOOL19 : 초소규모 
	- [Full CDM tools index (all tools, always current)](https://cdm.unfccc.int/methodologies/PAmethodologies/tools/)

## PACM 
**1. Standard: Demonstration of additionality in mechanism methodologies**

- Article 6.4 감독기구(Supervisory Body, SBM)가 2025년에 채택
- CDM의 investment analysis, barrier analysis, common practice analysis 접근을 계승하되 PACM 체계에 맞게 재구성한 것
- 초안 버전 링크(v02.0): [https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/A6.4-MEP004-A02_0.pdf](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/A6.4-MEP004-A02_0.pdf)
- 최종 채택본은 UNFCCC Article 6.4 공식 페이지에서 확인 필요: [https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-paris-agreement/article-64-mechanism](https://unfccc.int/process-and-meetings/the-paris-agreement/article-64-mechanism)

**2. Investment Analysis Tool**

- 첫 PACM 방법론(A6.4-AMM-001: Flaring or use of landfill gas, 2025년 10월 승인)과 함께 PACM은 프로젝트 개발자가 탄소배출권 수익 없이는 사업성이 없다는 것을 보여주도록 요구하는 투자 분석 도구를 채택했습니다
